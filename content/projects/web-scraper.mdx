## Overview

A custom scraper for collecting and analyzing information. It reaches sites either through Selenium or through custom HTTP requests to their pages and APIs. On some sites, after a deep dive, I uncovered their more private APIs, which let me scrape much more efficiently.

## Challenges

### Runtime

With basic methods like Selenium or plain HTTP requests, the volume of data can take a couple of hours to scrape. I got much better efficiency once I found loopholes that gave direct access to the sites' data.
