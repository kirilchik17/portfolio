## Overview

A custom scraper for collecting and analyzing information from Telegram. It is planned for news aggregation and for trade analysis of open trading channels.

## Challenges

### MTProto

Telegram load-balances its traffic across private servers and uses a custom protocol, MTProto, to move data efficiently. Combined with the protocol's poor documentation, that meant a long, tedious read through Telegram's docs plus a lot of experiments. It worked, but it was very difficult.
