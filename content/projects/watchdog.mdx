## Overview

My first project, built when I started working at Infoneto at 17. On first run and configuration it updated the registry for its own use and then ran as a service that monitored the configured programs. On any error or freeze it acted to get the program back on its feet.

## Challenges

### Execution

As my first project at the company, it tested my independence and ability to learn. I could ask questions, but the research, architecture, and programming were mine alone. I finished it in 3 days, against a 2-week deadline.
